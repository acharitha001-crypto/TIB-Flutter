# User Guide

1. **First launch** – the dashboard is empty. Open **Settings → Load demo data** to try the app quickly.
2. **Add a task** – tap **Add Task**, enter a title, choose a subject, due date and priority, then **Save Task**.
3. **Complete a task** – tick the checkbox. The completion ring on the dashboard updates.
4. **Edit or delete** – tap a task to edit it; swipe it left in the Planner to delete (tap **UNDO** to restore).
5. **Search and filter** – use the search box or the subject chips in the Planner.
6. **Add marks** – open **Performance → Add Marks**, pick subject, exam, marks and maximum marks.
7. **Set your target** – move the **Target score** slider in Settings. Subjects below it are shown in red.
8. **Dark mode** – switch it on in Settings.
